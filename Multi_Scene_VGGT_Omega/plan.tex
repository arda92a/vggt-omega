\begin{keybox}[Summary]
\textbf{Multi-Scene \VGGT} takes a bag of frames from an unknown number of scenes and returns,
for every scene it finds, the frame indices, camera poses relative to an anchor frame, and a
point cloud. The 16 register tokens of \VGGT{} serve as scene tokens. A \emph{route} head
clusters the frames in the middle of the backbone, so every later inter-frame attention runs
inside one scene. A second head and one masked scene-attention block refine the grouping at
the output, and the camera head runs once per scene. Training has two stages: affinity heads
first, then the scene block and the camera and depth heads, with the backbone frozen throughout.
\end{keybox}

% ============================================================
\section{Architecture}
% ============================================================

\subsection{Tokens}
\VGGT{} already appends one camera token and $16$ register tokens to the DINO patch tokens of
every frame. The register tokens are the scene tokens $\zscene$, so nothing is added to the
sequence:
\begin{equation}
  \bm{z}_i = \big[\, \zcam^{(i)} \,;\, \zscene^{(i)} \,;\, \zdino^{(i)} \,\big]
  \in \mathbb{R}^{(1 + 16 + HW) \times C}.
\end{equation}
The released model keeps the camera token of the first frame distinct from the others. In a
bag of several scenes only one frame carries it, which is why the pose loss is gauge-invariant
(Section~\ref{sec:loss}).

\subsection{Routing in the backbone}
The $24$ alternating-attention blocks are unchanged up to block $\ell_r$ (default $\ell_r = 11$).
After it, the \textbf{route head} reads the scene tokens of all frames, predicts the pairwise
affinity (below), and clusters the frames. In blocks $\ell_r + 1,\dots,23$ the inter-frame
attention, both the global and the register variant, runs on each group separately. This is
exactly a block-diagonal attention mask, but every group keeps dense attention. Frame
attention is per frame already. The dense head reads only patch tokens and is per frame, so
depth is isolated as well. Because later layers only ever see one scene, the frozen backbone
produces the features it was trained to produce for a single scene.

\subsection{Affinity head}
\label{sec:affinity}
Each frame gets a descriptor from the mean of its $16$ scene tokens,
\begin{equation}
  \bm{d}_i = W\,\mathrm{LN}\!\Big(\tfrac{1}{16}\sum_{k=1}^{16}\zscene^{(i)}[k]\Big) \in \mathbb{R}^{256}.
\end{equation}
The pair logit is an MLP on symmetric features,
\begin{equation}
  s_{ij} = \mathrm{MLP}\big(\,[\,|\bm{d}_i - \bm{d}_j| \,;\, \bm{d}_i \odot \bm{d}_j\,]\,\big),
  \qquad \hat A_{ij} = \sigma(s_{ij}), \quad \hat A_{ii} = 1 .
\end{equation}
A single linear layer on $[\bm{h}_i;\bm{h}_j]$ would give $s_{ij} = a(\bm{h}_i) + b(\bm{h}_j)$, which
cannot express ``same scene''. The last layer is initialised to a large positive bias, so an
untrained head puts every frame in one group and a single-scene input reproduces the original
model.

\subsection{Grouping}
Frames are grouped by \textbf{average-linkage agglomerative clustering} of $\hat{\bm{A}}$: the
two clusters with the highest mean affinity merge while that mean is at least $\tau$ (default
$0.5$). Connected components of $\hat A \ge \tau$ would merge two scenes through one false
positive pair. Singletons are allowed. Group ids start at $0$ and follow the first frame of
each group.

\subsection{Scene attention and final affinity (block 25)}
After block $24$ a second affinity head (same form, own weights) predicts $\hat{\bm{A}}$ again. The
groups, either predicted or ground truth (Section~\ref{sec:teacher}), define a boolean mask
$\bm{M}\in\{0,1\}^{N\times N}$, expanded to token level as $\bm{M}\otimes\bm{1}_{16\times16}$:
\begin{equation}
  \bm{W} = \mathrm{Softmax}\!\left(\frac{\bm{Q}\bm{K}^{\top}}{\sqrt{d_k}} + \log \bm{M}\otimes\bm{1}_{16\times 16}\right),
  \qquad \bm{Q},\bm{K},\bm{V}\in\mathbb{R}^{16N\times d_k}.
\end{equation}
Entries with $M_{ij}=0$ get weight exactly $0$. The diagonal is always open. The output
projection and the MLP output are zero-initialised. The affinity is read once more after this
block, which is the third supervised affinity. The mask is hard in training and inference, so
there is no soft-to-hard gap.

\subsection{Per-scene output}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The camera head runs once per group on that group's camera and register tokens.
  \item Each group has an \textbf{anchor}: the frame with the highest mean affinity inside the group.
        Camera poses are expressed relative to it, and the point cloud is unprojected from depth
        and expressed in the anchor camera frame.
\end{itemize}
\begin{tcolorbox}[enhanced, colback=softgray, colframe=rulegray, boxrule=0.5pt, arc=2pt,
                  left=8pt, right=8pt, top=4pt, bottom=4pt]
\begin{verbatim}
scenes = model.predict_scenes(images)      # images: (N, 3, H, W)
scenes = [
    {"images": [0, 3, 4], "anchor": 3,
     "camera_poses": (3, 3, 4),            # camera-from-anchor, anchor = identity
     "pointcloud":   (3, H, W, 3)},        # anchor camera frame
    {"images": [1, 2, 5], ...},
]
\end{verbatim}
\end{tcolorbox}

% ============================================================
\section{Losses}
\label{sec:loss}
% ============================================================
Every term is computed per ground-truth scene and averaged over scenes, so no frame pair or
depth reduction crosses a scene boundary.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \small
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.35}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Term} & \textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Definition} \\
    \midrule
    Affinity & $\Lall_{\text{aff}}$ & Class-balanced BCE on off-diagonal pairs, for the route, final, and post-attention affinity. \\
    Geometry & $\Lall_{\text{geom}}$ & Depth L1 per scene, plus L1 on 3D points in the anchor camera frame. \\
    Pose     & $\Lall_{\text{pose}}$ & Rotation, translation, FoV and pairwise relative pose, after anchoring prediction and ground truth to the same frame. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\subsection{Affinity}
With $P$ the positive and $N$ the negative off-diagonal pairs,
\begin{equation}
  \Lall_{\text{aff}} = \tfrac12\Big(\tfrac{1}{|P|}\sum_{(i,j)\in P}\ell_{ij} + \tfrac{1}{|N|}\sum_{(i,j)\in N}\ell_{ij}\Big),
  \qquad \ell_{ij} = \mathrm{BCE}(s_{ij}, A_{ij}).
\end{equation}
With $S$ scenes only a $1/S$ share of pairs is positive and the diagonal is trivially $1$, so a
plain mean rewards ``everything is one scene''. A bag with one scene has no negatives and uses
the positive term alone.

\subsection{Pose}
The network predicts the cameras of each scene in its own gauge: nothing marks which frame is the
origin. Both sides are therefore re-expressed relative to the first frame $a$ of the scene slice,
$E_i' = E_i E_a^{-1}$, before the loss. A rigid change of the predicted world frame costs zero,
and the frame $a$ itself carries no loss.
\begin{equation}
  \Lall_{\text{pose}} = \Lall_{\text{rot}} + \lambda_t \Lall_{\text{trans}} + \lambda_f \Lall_{\text{fov}} + \lambda_p \Lall_{\text{pair}} .
\end{equation}

\subsection{Total}
\begin{equation}
  \Lall = \lambda_1 \big(\Lall_{\text{aff}}^{\text{final}} + \lambda_r \Lall_{\text{aff}}^{\text{route}} + \Lall_{\text{aff}}^{\text{out}}\big)
        + \lambda_2 \Lall_{\text{geom}} + \lambda_3 \Lall_{\text{pose}},
\end{equation}
with $\lambda_1=\lambda_2=\lambda_3=\lambda_r=1$ in the shipped configs.

% ============================================================
\section{Training}
% ============================================================

\subsection{Mixed-scene bags}
Each step is one bag of $S$ scenes. $S \in \{1,2,3,4\}$ is drawn with weights $(0.1, 0.35, 0.3, 0.25)$,
and $S=1$ teaches the model to leave one scene alone. Scenes come from distinct sequences. Every
scene is cropped to a random $K \in [3, 6]$ frames, the bag is capped at $16$ frames, and the
frames are \textbf{shuffled}, so position carries no scene information. With probability $0.5$ all scenes
come from one dataset, which gives harder negatives than mixing unrelated datasets.

\subsection{Teacher forcing}
\label{sec:teacher}
Early on the predicted groups are poor, so with probability $p(t)$ the route, scene attention and
camera head use ground-truth groups instead. $p$ is annealed linearly from $1.0$ to $0.2$ in
stage 1 and from $0.5$ to $0$ in stage 2. At the end of training the model runs on its own groups,
as at test time.

\subsection{Stages}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Stage 1: affinity.} The backbone, camera head, depth head and scene attention are
        frozen. The route and final affinity heads train with $\Lall_{\text{aff}}$.
  \item \textbf{Stage 2: scene block, camera and depth.} Only the backbone is frozen. Scene attention,
        both affinity heads, the camera head and the depth head train with $\Lall$ at a lower learning rate.
\end{enumerate}
Keeping the backbone frozen is deliberate: with routing, it sees one scene at a time, which is the
distribution it was trained on.

\subsection{Data}
ETH3D and Replica are converted to the unified layout and merged under one root, one folder per scene
and named \texttt{eth3d\_*} or \texttt{replica\_*}. The split is by scene, never by frame:
\texttt{training/dataset\_preparation/split\_sequences.py} checks every scene, then holds out the same
number of scenes from each source into \texttt{val.txt} and keeps the rest in \texttt{train.txt}
(fixed seed). Training reads \texttt{train.txt}, validation reads \texttt{val.txt}. Neither dataset is used
by \texttt{eval/}, which now covers Sintel only, so no evaluation scene is seen in training.

% ============================================================
\section{Evaluation and Logging}
% ============================================================
Validation runs on held-out mixed bags after every epoch, and once before training. Four systems
are scored on the same bags:

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \small
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l X@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{System} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
    \midrule
    predicted & Inference: predicted groups. \\
    oracle    & Same model with ground-truth groups. \\
    plain     & No isolation, every frame in one group. \\
    separate  & Each scene forwarded alone, the original model (stage 2 only). \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Clustering:} ARI, pair F1, pair agreement, exact partition rate, scene-count error,
        for the final and the route head.
  \item \textbf{Pose:} relative rotation and translation error in degrees, RRA and RTA at $5^\circ$
        and $15^\circ$, AUC@30, on frame pairs inside a ground-truth scene (gauge-independent).
  \item \textbf{Depth:} abs-rel and $\delta<1.25$ with per-scene median scaling, and abs-rel without scaling.
  \item \textbf{Gaps:} \texttt{contamination} $=$ plain $-$ oracle, the cost of mixing scenes.
        \texttt{cluster\_gap} $=$ predicted $-$ oracle, the cost of clustering errors.
\end{itemize}
Everything goes to Weights \& Biases: \texttt{train\_step/*} per step, \texttt{train/*} and
\texttt{val/<system>/*} per epoch, an affinity heat-map (final, route, target) per epoch, and
best values in the run summary.

\subsection{Ablations for the paper}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item No isolation (\texttt{plain}) against routed (\texttt{predicted}).
  \item Route layer $\ell_r \in \{5, 11, 17\}$.
  \item Average, single and complete linkage.
  \item Route head only, final head only, both.
  \item Teacher-forcing schedule: none, constant, annealed.
  \item Number of scenes $S$ and frames $K$ at test time, beyond the training range.
\end{enumerate}
